Quit NRT

Log your NRT, see what sets off cravings, and taper to zero a week at a time.

Before you start

  • This app isn’t medical advice. It helps you keep track and plan. It can’t tell you what’s right for your body.
  • Talk to a pharmacist or GP before you change how you use NRT, especially if you’re pregnant, breastfeeding or living with a heart condition.
  • NRT for longer is far safer than smoking. If cutting down gets hard, you can slow the plan down.
Everything you log stays on this phone. The app has no internet access.
