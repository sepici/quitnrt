# UK retailer NRT stock summary (researched Oct 2026)

These are snapshots from retailer and aggregator listings, so stock varies by branch and day. "Own brand" means retailer-labelled NRT; the detailed entries are in `retailers_uk.json`.

**Pattern:** every supermarket own-label line found (Asda, Morrisons, Sainsbury's, Tesco), plus Superdrug's, is the same basic pair:
- peppermint gum, 2 mg and 4 mg, 96 pieces, max 15 a day
- peppermint lozenges, 2 mg and 4 mg (chosen by time to first cigarette), 72 or 144, max 15 a day

Asda and Morrisons packs say "made by Wrafton Laboratories" (Perrigo), which also makes the **Galpharm Nicotine Replace** value brand. No retailer own-label patches, mini lozenges, mouth sprays or inhalators were found, except Boots NicAssist.

## NHS stop-smoking services (not shops)
- **England:** the community-pharmacy Smoking Cessation Service supplies NRT free under generic names from the Drug Tariff Part VIIIC list. That list covers gum, lozenges, patches, inhalator cartridges, nasal spray, mouth spray and sublingual tablets, so the brand depends on the pharmacy's stock.
- **Scotland:** NRT is free from pharmacies, advisers and GPs (NHS Inform).
- **Products a user may get through NHS but rarely in shops:**
  - **Nicotine nasal spray:** few shops stock it; Boots NicAssist and Nicorette exist but stock is patchy.
  - **Inhalator:** the Boots NicAssist inhalator is discontinued, so the Nicorette Inhalator is the only one left.
  - **Microtabs.**
  - **Non-NRT options to keep out of the nicotine catalogue:** varenicline, cytisine (cytisinicline) and bupropion on prescription; free vape starter kits in some areas ("Swap to Stop").
- **Ownership change:** Haleon's Nicotinell business outside the US was sold to Dr Reddy's in Sept 2024. dm+d now lists Nicotinell items (e.g. Nicotinell Rapid Relief 1 mg mouth spray) under Dr Reddy's Laboratories (UK) Ltd.
